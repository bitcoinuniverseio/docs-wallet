## Can Universe recover my wallet if I lose the phrase?

No. Not for anyone, under any circumstances. There is no account, no reset email, and no support
override. Anyone offering to do it is arranging a second theft.

## Can Universe reverse a transaction?

No. Nobody can, including miners and the recipient. A confirmed Bitcoin transaction is final.

## Does Universe hold my keys?

No. They are generated on your device, encrypted with your password, and never leave the extension.

## Why is my available balance lower than my total?

Coins carrying assets, or coins the wallet could not check, are held back from ordinary payments. See
[Protected outputs](/docs-wallet/concepts/protected-outputs).

## Why does a screen say the operation is not available?

Your build does not carry evidence for that operation. It is not an outage, a permission, or a
setting. See [Why an action is unavailable](/docs-wallet/assets/why-unavailable).

## How many protocols are supported?

The registry holds {snapshot.protocolCount}. Universe Wallet {snapshot.walletVersion} authorizes{' '}
{snapshot.supportedProtocolCount} of them. See
[What this release authorizes](/docs-wallet/assets/support-state).

## Can I speed up a stuck transaction?

Not from inside Universe Wallet. There is no fee bump, no speed up, and no child-pays-for-parent. You
can mark a transaction replaceable when you create it, and the wallet does not give you a way to build
the replacement. See [Read your activity](/docs-wallet/tasks/activity).

## Can I sign a message of my own?

No. Message signing appears only as an approval, in response to a request from a connected site. There
is no compose-and-sign screen.

## Can I connect a Ledger or Trezor?

No. They appear in the hardware list, disabled, marked coming soon. Keystone is the working
integration. See [Hardware wallets](/docs-wallet/tasks/hardware-wallet).

## Which version am I running?

Settings, then About. Read [Which version you are running](/docs-wallet/start/versions) before you
compare it to anything on this site, because the published store build predates most of what is
documented here.

## Does connecting a site let it spend my coins?

No. Connecting lets a site see one address on one network and ask you for signatures. Every signature
is approved separately. See [Connection permissions](/docs-wallet/concepts/connections).

## I cancelled a listing. Is it really cancelled?

Removing a listing removes it from that marketplace. It does not revoke your signature. Anyone holding
a copy can still complete it while the coin is unspent. The only certain revocation is to spend the
coin yourself. See [What a signature authorizes](/docs-wallet/concepts/what-a-signature-authorizes).

## Is my activity private?

No. Chain data is public by nature, and no wallet setting changes that. See
[Privacy](/docs-wallet/safety/privacy).

## Can I use this on my phone?

No. Browser extensions do not run on mobile Chrome or Safari.

## Does it work in Firefox?

Not as a published extension. Build targets exist in the source tree; no listing exists.

## Why are there no screenshots on this site?

Because there are no real ones to use. The product's capture harness currently runs the wallet
offline, so every screen it captures settles into an empty or error state. Presenting a mock as a real
capture would be a false claim, so this site uses hand-authored diagrams and describes interfaces in
words instead. See [Diagram conventions](/docs-wallet/reference/diagram-conventions).

## Will support ever ask for my recovery phrase?

No. Nobody legitimate ever will, in any wallet, for any reason. See
[Phishing and impostors](/docs-wallet/safety/phishing).
