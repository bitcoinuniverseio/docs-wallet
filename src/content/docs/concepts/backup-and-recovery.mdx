Your coins are not in the extension. They are on the chain. The extension holds the keys that
authorize moving them. A compatible wallet can reproduce phrase-derived keys from the same recovery details.

## What the phrase restores

The phrase restores keys derived from that phrase when the receiving wallet uses the same passphrase, derivation paths, network and address formats. BIP-39 compatibility alone does not establish support for every account or product.

Separately imported keys and hardware-wallet keys require their own backups. This page does not promise seed-only recovery of Lightning channel state, Taproot Assets proofs and databases, Spark recovery data or mobile pairing state.

## What the phrase does not restore

Everything that is a setting:

- your password,
- connected sites,
- address labels and contacts,
- coins you locked yourself,
- spending limits.

These settings live on the device. After restoring keys, check known addresses, the selected network and authoritative balances before using the wallet. Restore separate product backups where required. Missing history or preferences does not by itself prove that funds were lost.

## The five situations

| What happened | Recoverable | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forgot the password | Yes | The password unlocks one installation and is not the backup. Reinstall, then restore from the phrase. |
| Device lost, stolen, or broken | Yes | Restore from the phrase on another device. Your coins were never on the old one. |
| Extension removed or profile wiped | Yes | Reinstalling gives you an empty wallet. Restore from the phrase. |
| Someone else saw the phrase | Access is fine, secrecy is not | Move everything to a wallet made from a **new** phrase, immediately. See [If your wallet is compromised](/docs-wallet/safety/compromised-wallet). |
| Phrase lost and you cannot unlock | **No** | There is no path. See below. |

<Aside type="danger" title="The one with no way back">
If your recovery phrase is gone and you can no longer unlock the wallet, nobody can recover it. Not
Universe, not the browser vendor, not a support agent, not a recovery service. Anyone offering to do
it is arranging a second theft. There is no exception to this and there never will be.
</Aside>

## The situation people miss

**Phrase lost, but the wallet still unlocks.** This is urgent and it is survivable, and people sit on
it for months because nothing appears to be wrong.

Do one of these today:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Export recovery phrase**, and write it down properly. See
   [Back up your recovery phrase](/docs-wallet/start/back-up).
2. Or move everything to a wallet whose phrase you do have.

The window closes the moment anything happens to that browser profile.

## Restoring, step by step

1. Install the extension from the real store listing. See [Install](/docs-wallet/start/install).
2. Choose to import a wallet.
3. Enter the phrase, in order, plus the BIP-39 passphrase if you used one.
4. Select the address type you used before. If the balance looks wrong, this is almost always why. See
   [Address types](/docs-wallet/concepts/address-types).
5. Confirm a known address of yours appears.
6. Set your password, auto-lock, and any settings you want back.

## Interrupted operations and device pairing

A storage error or missing reply does not prove that a payment failed. Keep the original operation reference and check that operation instead of creating another payment. Do not erase unresolved history to clear an error.

Development work on authenticated mobile metadata receipts and interrupted-pairing recovery does not mean mobile pairing is publicly released. Pairing keys, replay counters and metadata history are not recreated by the recovery phrase. Do not copy an old counter backward or reuse an old pairing key after a reset. Public capabilities remain those listed by the current release, not those present only in source.
