**Intended reader:** someone making their first outgoing payment from Universe Wallet.
**Goal:** an amount arriving at someone else's address, with no asset spent by accident.
**Prerequisites:** a funded wallet, a backed-up phrase, and the recipient's address.
**Chain and network:** Bitcoin mainnet in this example.
**Safety:** this is irreversible. Nothing on this page can undo a confirmed transaction.

<Aside type="caution" title="Sending is behind a release gate">
The send and broadcast screens sit behind the `bitcoin/send` and `bitcoin/broadcast` gates. Whether
they work in your build was decided when that build was released, not by this documentation. If the
screen tells you the operation is not available in your build, it is not available, and no setting
changes that. See [Why an action is unavailable](/docs-wallet/assets/why-unavailable).
</Aside>

## Two facts first

1. **Coins are spent whole.** Paying 0.02 BTC from a 0.03 BTC coin does not leave that coin partly
   spent. The whole coin is consumed and the remainder comes back to you as a change output. See
   [Inputs, outputs, fees, change](/docs-wallet/concepts/inputs-outputs-fees).
2. **The fee follows size, not amount.** Sending 10 BTC costs the same as sending 0.001 BTC if the
   transaction is the same size in bytes.

## Steps

1. Send a **test amount** first. Small enough that losing it would be annoying rather than serious.
2. Open **Send**, and confirm the account and network at the top of the screen.
3. Paste the recipient address. Compare the first four and last four characters against the source.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Choose a fee. See [Choose a fee](/docs-wallet/tasks/choose-a-fee).
6. **Read the review screen.** Do not skim it. See
   [Review a transaction](/docs-wallet/tasks/review-a-transaction).
7. Approve, or reject. Rejecting costs nothing.
8. Wait for the test amount to arrive at the other end and be confirmed.
9. Only then send the real amount.

## Expected result

The transaction appears in **Activity** as pending, then gains confirmations. Your available balance
drops by the amount plus the fee. A change output returns to an address you own.

## How to verify

- The recipient confirms arrival of the test amount.
- Your activity entry shows the fee you chose, not a larger one.
- Your total balance dropped by amount plus fee, and no asset you hold has moved.

## Common failures

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not enough available balance, though the total looks fine | Some coins are protected and cannot fund a plain payment | See [Protected outputs](/docs-wallet/concepts/protected-outputs). |
| The transaction stays pending for a long time | The fee rate was below what the network is currently clearing | Wait. See the warning below about fee bumping. |
| The change output is missing on the review | The remainder is being paid entirely as fee | Reject. Do not approve a transaction whose change line you cannot account for. |
| The screen says the operation is not available in this build | The release did not authorize it | Nothing you can change. See [Why an action is unavailable](/docs-wallet/assets/why-unavailable). |

<Aside type="danger" title="There is no way to speed up a stuck transaction from inside the wallet">
You can mark a transaction as replaceable when you create it, and that flag is off by default. But
Universe Wallet 1.7.5.8 has **no fee bump, no speed up, and no child-pays-for-parent flow**. Marking a
transaction replaceable does not give you a way to replace it here. If a payment is stuck, your
options are to wait, or to use separate software that can build a replacement. Choose the fee
carefully the first time.
</Aside>

## Recovery path

There is none after confirmation. Before broadcast, rejecting the approval is free and complete. That
is why the review screen is the last real safety control you have, and why it is worth the thirty
seconds.
