Review a transaction or PSBT before signing

About 10 minutesIrreversible if wrongWallet 1.7.5.8· unpublished

Outcome: You can read a transaction review or PSBT screen and sign only what you verified.

Completion looks like: You can name what a signature commits to before any approval.

If something goes wrong: Refusing is always available and always safe. No signature, no consequence.
