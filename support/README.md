# Support

How to get help, report a bug, or disclose a vulnerability.

## Before you write

Most issues resolve in minutes with [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/README.md). When you do write, include the diagnostics the product prepares for you: **Copy support bundle** from the About screen (version and endpoints), and **Copy report** from any blocking screen.

## Bugs and feature requests

Open an issue at [github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/wallet/issues](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/wallet/issues). State what you did, what you expected, and what happened. The **Feedback** entry in Settings opens the same tracker with your context pre-filled.

## Security vulnerabilities

Email `legal@bitcoinuniverse.io` with the details. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability; give us the chance to fix it before disclosure. Include steps to reproduce and the version from the About screen.

## The one rule of safe support

No legitimate helper, in any channel, ever needs your recovery phrase, private key, or password. The moment anyone asks, you are talking to a thief; see [If your wallet is compromised](../security-and-recovery/compromised-wallet.md) if you already answered.

## Frequently asked

**Is Universe Wallet self-custody?** Yes. Keys are generated and stored on your device; Universe cannot move or recover funds. See [Security model](../security-and-recovery/security-model.md).

**Why is a feature missing that another install has?** Releases enable protocol actions only with current evidence; your version or its dependencies decide what shows. See [Supported protocols](../assets-and-protocols/supported-protocols.md).

**Can Universe reverse my transaction?** No one can reverse a confirmed transaction.

**Where are the terms and privacy policy?** [bitcoinuniverse.io/terms](https://bitcoinuniverse.io/terms) and [bitcoinuniverse.io/privacy](https://bitcoinuniverse.io/privacy).
