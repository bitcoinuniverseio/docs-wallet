## Printable checklists
 
### 1 · Suspected compromised wallet
 
- Stop signing and approving anything on the suspect device. 
- Do not send a "test transaction" from it. 
- On a clean device, create a fresh wallet and write its phrase on paper. 
- Move remaining funds and artifacts to the new wallet. 
- Remove the old wallet from the suspect device and retire the old phrase for life. 
 Full guide 

 
### 2 · Phishing and fake support
 
- Nobody legitimate ever needs your recovery words. Not support, not "validation", not upgrades. 
- Nobody legitimate needs a test signature, a screen share, or a hurry. 
- Check the origin on every approval. Look-alike domains are the con. 
- Download extensions only from the official listing. 
 Full guide 

 
### 3 · Recovery and backup boundaries
 
- Your phrase restores your keys. Anyone holding it holds your money. 
- Your password unlocks this device only. It cannot restore funds. 
- Lost password with an intact phrase: recoverable. Lost phrase with a forgotten password: not. 
 Full guide 

 
### 4 · Version identification
 
- Settings → About shows your version. 
- Store build 1.0.13 (April 2025) differs from the documented 1.7.5.8 source tree. 
- Where this site and your wallet disagree, your wallet is correct. 
 Full guide 

 
### 5 · Transaction review checklist
 
- Every output accounted for: payment + change + fee. 
- Change returns to your wallet. A stranger holding your change is theft in progress. 
- Fee far above the recommended rate: stop and reread. 
- Unreadable data signatures: treat the length warning as a stop sign. 
 Full guide · Safety Lab 

 
### 6 · Official support and reporting
 
- Documentation issues: docs-wallet issues 
- Security reports: see the repository's security policy 
- The wallet will never direct you to a support agent who asks for your phrase.
