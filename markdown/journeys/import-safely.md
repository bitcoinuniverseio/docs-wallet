# Import a wallet safely
 About 10 minutes Irreversible if wrong Wallet 1.7.5.8 · unpublished 
 Outcome: Existing keys are restored into this wallet without the phrase touching anything networked.
 Before you start: Your recovery phrase or private key, already written down
 Completion looks like: The restored wallet shows the expected account and balances (or an empty account for a fresh phrase).
 If something goes wrong: Import copies; it does not move. The original phrase keeps working and must stay protected.
 
 
## All steps
 
- 
### Choose the import source
 You are trying to Select where the keys come from. You should see A list of sources as selectable cards. You do Pick the source matching your phrase (for example a 12-word Universe-compatible wallet). Before you continue, check The source card names the wallet family the phrase belongs to. What can go wrong Wrong source means empty balances. Nothing is lost; redo with the right source. Reversible? Yes. 
- 
### Enter the phrase
 You are trying to Restore keys from your offline copy. You should see A grid of twelve masked inputs. You do Type words from paper. Keep secure input on. Before you continue, check The words came from paper, not from a file, a photo, or a message someone sent you. What can go wrong A phrase someone gave you 'to import' is their lever on your money. Import only phrases you generated. Reversible? No. Read twice. 
- 
### Verify what was restored
 You are trying to Confirm the account and address type match your old wallet. You should see Home shows the restored account. You do Check the receive address against the old wallet's address where possible. Before you continue, check Address type mismatch is the usual cause of 'my funds are gone' after import. They are on another derivation path. What can go wrong If balances look missing, stop sending. Recheck the address type before anything else. Reversible? Yes.
