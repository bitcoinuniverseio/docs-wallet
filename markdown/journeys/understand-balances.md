## All steps

### Read the home balance anatomy
 You are trying to See total, available, and held-back amounts with the ownership line. You should see Home splits the balance when parts are not spendable. You do Compare total and available. The difference is explained below the numbers. Before you continue, check Unconfirmed incoming payments and protected outputs both reduce available funds. What can go wrong Treating the total as spendable leads to stuck payments, not losses. Reversible? Yes. 
- 
### Open protected outputs
 You are trying to See what the wallet holds back and why. You should see The protected outputs screen lists held outputs with reasons. You do Open it from settings or home. Before you continue, check Protected outputs carry artifacts (inscriptions, rare sats) that plain spending would destroy or move. What can go wrong Forcing a spend that consumes a protected output can send your art with your payment. Reversible? Yes.
