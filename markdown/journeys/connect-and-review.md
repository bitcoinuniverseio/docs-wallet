# Connect to a site and review requested access
 About 8 minutes Check before you act Wallet 1.7.5.8 · unpublished 
 Outcome: You can connect a site deliberately and read what the connection actually grants.
 Before you start: A site you have decided to trust
 Completion looks like: The site shows as connected in the wallet, and you can name what it may and may not do.
 If something goes wrong: Disconnecting ends access instantly. Connections grant identity visibility, never spending on their own.
 
 
## All steps
 
- 
### Start the connection from the site
 You are trying to Trigger the wallet's connection approval. You should see The wallet opens an approval naming the site's origin. You do Click the site's Connect button. Before you continue, check Read the origin in the approval. example-site.test-style names or look-alike domains mean close it. What can go wrong Connecting on a phishing page exposes your account identity to attackers, who then tailor the scam. Reversible? Yes. 
- 
### Read the request
 You are trying to Know what access is asked. You should see The approval states the account access requested. You do Approve or reject. Before you continue, check A connection shows your account to the site. It does not authorize spending; signatures are separate approvals. What can go wrong Approving unknown origins trains you to click through approvals, which is the habit scams harvest. Reversible? Yes. 
- 
### Know the unverified-origin gate
 You are trying to Recognize the state the wallet shows when it cannot verify a site. You should see A full gate: 'Unable to verify this site', with no approve control. You do Nothing to click. Close it. Before you continue, check The gate appears when the wallet's site check cannot complete. It fails closed on purpose. What can go wrong No third party can legitimately 'unlock' this gate. Instructions claiming to are the scam. Reversible? Yes.
