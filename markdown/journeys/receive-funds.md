# Receive funds for the first time
 About 8 minutes Check before you act Wallet 1.7.5.8 · unpublished 
 Outcome: You requested your first payment to an address you verified in your own wallet.
 Before you start: A wallet created or imported
 Completion looks like: The payment appears in activity with its confirmation state.
 If something goes wrong: Receiving is reversible by simply sending onward. Nothing broadcast here spends your funds.
 
 
## All steps
 
- 
### Open Receive
 You are trying to Get your receiving address. You should see The receive screen reports it is ready to receive. You do Open Receive from home. Before you continue, check The network shown in the header matches the network the payer expects. Testnet coins and mainnet coins are not interchangeable. What can go wrong Sending mainnet funds to a testnet address loses them. Read the network label. Reversible? Yes. 
- 
### Share the address
 You are trying to Give the payer your address or QR. You should see An address string and a QR code. You do Copy, or show the QR, or request via a payment name if configured. Before you continue, check Paste-back check: paste the address where you will send it to yourself and compare the first and last 6 characters. What can go wrong Clipboard malware swaps addresses. The paste-back check defeats the common ones. Reversible? Yes. 
- 
### Watch it arrive
 You are trying to See the payment and its confirmation state. You should see Activity lists the incoming payment; confirmations count up. You do Open Activity. Nothing else to do. Before you continue, check A payment is spendable per the wallet's confirmation policy; large payments deserve more confirmations before you ship goods. What can go wrong If nothing appears, verify the address the payer actually used. A wrong network or old address is the usual cause. Reversible? Yes.
