# Create a wallet and secure its recovery material
 About 15 minutes Irreversible if wrong Wallet 1.7.5.8 · unpublished 
 Outcome: A new wallet exists, its recovery phrase is backed up offline, and the backup status says so.
 Before you start: A private space where no camera can see your screen
 Completion looks like: Settings shows the wallet backed up, and the phrase exists only offline in your keeping.
 If something goes wrong: Until you send funds in, everything here is re-doable. Lose the phrase after funding and the money is gone; there is no reset.
 
 
## All steps
 
- 
### Create the device password
 You are trying to Set the password that unlocks this wallet on this device. You should see Two password fields and a Continue control. You do Enter a strong password twice and continue. Before you continue, check You understand this password unlocks this device only. It is not the recovery phrase and cannot restore funds. What can go wrong A forgotten password with no phrase means reinstalling from scratch. The phrase is the real key. Reversible? No. Read twice. 
- 
### Choose the address type
 You are trying to Pick the address model for the wallet. You should see A list of address type cards; Continue stays disabled until a scan finishes. You do Keep the default unless you have a specific reason. Continue. Before you continue, check The default covers most users. Changing it later means recovery procedures. What can go wrong Importing into a mismatched address type makes balances appear missing. They are on the other path. Reversible? No. Read twice. 
- 
### Write the recovery phrase offline
 You are trying to Capture the 12 words where no camera or clipboard can reach them. You should see The phrase is shown once, masked by secure input. You do Write the words on paper in order. Verify by re-entering when asked. Before you continue, check Words exist on paper only. Never photographed, never typed into anything networked, never clipped. What can go wrong A phrase that touched a phone camera, cloud note, or password manager sync is a leaked key waiting to be used. Reversible? No. Read twice. 
- 
### Confirm backup status
 You are trying to The wallet records that the backup step is done. You should see Home shows the wallet; Settings shows backup status. You do Open Settings and confirm the backup entry reflects your paper copy. Before you continue, check Settings is the source of truth for backup state inside the wallet. What can go wrong Dismissing the backup reminder without writing the phrase is the most common permanent-loss story. Reversible? Yes.
