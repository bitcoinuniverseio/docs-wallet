# Switch chain or network safely
 About 6 minutes Check before you act Wallet 1.7.5.8 · unpublished 
 Outcome: You can move the wallet between chains and test networks without losing track of where money applies.
 Completion looks like: You switched the active chain deliberately and can switch back.
 If something goes wrong: Switching back restores the previous view. No funds move when you switch.

### Approve a switch request
