# A wallet you can read before you sign it
 Universe Wallet is a self-custody browser extension for Bitcoin, Dogecoin, Zcash, and Fractal
Bitcoin. It keeps coins that carry digital artifacts out of ordinary payments, and it puts a
plain-language account of what you are about to authorize in front of every signature.

## Read this before you trust anything else on this site
 The build published on the Chrome Web Store is 1.0.13, from 15 April 2025 . This
documentation is written against the 1.7.5.8 source tree, which has not
been submitted or published. If you installed from the store, you are running the older build,
and screens described here may not exist in it.

## What this documentation is for
 Section titled “What this documentation is for” 

 You are holding your own keys. That means no company can move your money, and equally that no company
can give it back. Every page here is written so that believing it cannot cost you anything: where a
capability could not be verified in the wallet’s source, this site says it is not available rather
than describing how it might work.

 
- 
### Install and set up
 Which browsers work, where the real extension lives, and how to create or import a wallet without losing it a week later.
 
- 
### What a signature authorizes
 The single most valuable page here. Four kinds of approval, and the exact boundary of each one.
 
- 
### Protected outputs
 Why some of your balance will not spend, and why that is the feature rather than the fault.
 
- 
### Review before you sign
 How to read the approval screen top to bottom, and which line to check when you are in a hurry.

### Backup and recovery
 Which loss your recovery phrase covers, which it does not, and the one situation with no way back.

### If something is wrong
 What to do first when you think your phrase was seen, a signature went wrong, or coins moved without you.
